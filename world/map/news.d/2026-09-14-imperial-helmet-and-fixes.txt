{title:Imperial Helmet, quest fixes and a lot of polish}
{date:2026-09-14}

The first content update since TMW turned 22 in April. The Mana client got its
0.8 release in August, and this update brings the server and the game data up
to date with the fixes that piled up over the summer.

{b}New content and changes{/b}
{ul}
{li}Cerhan, the blacksmith in the swamp, can now purify a Bull Helmet into the
  new Imperial Helmet, which trades the raw power for better protection. The
  Bull Helmet's critical bonus was lowered a little to match. (Ledmitz){/li}
{li}The slot machines keep their dialog open between pulls, so you no longer
  have to click the machine again for every coin (bjorn){/li}
{/ul}

{b}Fixes{/b}
{ul}
{li}Blue Sage quest: a server bug could mark the book, material and slime
  tasks as done for players who never did them, handing out the Grimoire
  early or blocking it. New quest variables now start clean. If your Blue
  Sage progress is in that state, ask a GM. (bjorn){/li}
{li}Silk Pants quest: players who left Crastur in the middle of his pirate
  task can continue the quest again (bjorn){/li}
{li}Closed a loophole that let level-restricted items be equipped below the
  required level (HoraK){/li}
{li}Pirates no longer get confused by stacked bandanas and hats, as part of
  the CRC 2026 event code cleanup (Hello=)){/li}
{li}The item weights shown by the client now match the server again for about
  a hundred items: rings, robes, sulfur powder, lifestone, bone knife and
  many more (Hello=), Ledmitz, Zinnia){/li}
{li}Item bonuses such as hit, critical, attack speed and max HP are declared
  the same way on every item, so all clients show them alike. The GM Amulet
  and the Dragon Sword had attributes no client could display. (bjorn){/li}
{li}Heathin's quest marker is hidden until you reach the required level
  (Mana client 0.8 and later) (bjorn){/li}
{li}The Troll's death animation no longer replays before it despawns
  (bjorn){/li}
{li}Ian's dialog got clickable links and a typo pass (bjorn){/li}
{/ul}

{author:bjorn}
