{title:Mana 0.8 is out!}
{date:2026-08-28}

A new release of the Mana client is out, one year after 0.7 brought it back
to life. It packs more than a hundred improvements, most of them aimed at
everyday play, for example:

{ul}
{li}Drag and drop items between all windows{/li}
{li}Health bars on monsters you fight{/li}
{li}See which servers are online before you connect{/li}
{li}Pick your language in the settings{/li}
{li}Alt+Enter toggles fullscreen{/li}
{li}Walking to an item stops next to it, and the pickup range is adjustable{/li}
{li}Being overweight no longer interrupts you with a dialog{/li}
{li}The Outfit window takes as many items as you like{/li}
{li}Many fixes and smaller improvements{/li}
{/ul}
